## 1. CTA Section Redesigned into a Split Layout with Social Proof Stats

**Old design:** The CTA banner was a centered, text-only block on a dark purple background with a single "Schedule a free consultation" button. No supporting context was present — just the headline and button.

**New design:** The CTA section is restructured into a two-column layout. The left side retains the headline and action buttons, while the right side surfaces three key social proof metrics: **300% Year-over-Year Growth**, **Global ERP Transformation Experience**, and **100% Client-Focused Approach** — each in its own card.

**Reasoning:** A CTA without supporting evidence asks visitors to act on trust alone. By placing compelling proof points alongside the call-to-action, the new design reduces hesitation and increases the likelihood of conversion. Split layouts also create better visual balance and reduce the cognitive heaviness of a full-width dark banner. This pattern is well-established in high-converting B2B landing pages.

## 2. Founder Profiles Added to the "Story of Highshine" Section

**Old design:** The "Story of Highshine" section showed two grey placeholder boxes on the left side, alongside the descriptive text and a blockquote. The placeholders conveyed no identity or credibility.

**New design:** Each placeholder was replaced with a dedicated founder profile card — one for **Hari Gautham (Founder & CEO)** and one for **Surendar (Co-Founder & COO)** — complete with a photo, name, title, and a short personal tagline. Below the cards, two stat chips highlight **300% year-over-year growth** and **post go-live commitment to every client**.

**Reasoning:** People buy from people. Replacing grey boxes with real founder portraits and titles immediately humanises the brand and builds trust. Named leadership with clear roles signals accountability and professionalism — especially critical in the B2B ERP consulting space where clients are making large, long-term investment decisions. The stat chips reinforce the narrative without requiring the reader to scroll elsewhere.

## 3. "Leadership & Culture" Section Enriched with Quantified Metrics and Categorised Tags

**Old design:** The Leadership & Culture section consisted of a team photo on the left and two text blocks ("The Leadership" and "Cultivating Growth") on the right. The content was descriptive but lacked any scannable proof or structure.

**New design:** The same section now includes a selfie-style team photo with an **"Employee-first culture"** badge overlay, two stat chips (**100% Employee First** and **4+ Training Programs**), a tag cluster (Collaborative workspaces, Continuous training, Employee welfare, Knowledge sharing), and two CTAs: **Meet the team** and **Our values**.

**Reasoning:** Culture claims without evidence are generic and forgettable. By quantifying culture commitments (e.g., "4+ training programs") and surfacing them visually, the new design makes the section scannable and credible. The tag chips reduce reading effort for busy visitors, while the dual CTAs give both hiring audiences (prospective employees) and client audiences (who care about team quality) clear next steps tailored to their intent.

## 4. "Inside the World of Highshine" Gallery Enhanced with Swiper Carousel

**Old design:** The gallery displayed five team photos in a curved fan layout under the headline "Inside the World of Highshine — Our People, Our Story." While visually unique, some images were partially hidden and difficult to view clearly.

**New design:** The gallery now uses a responsive Swiper carousel showing three images at a time, allowing every photo to be viewed clearly without overlap. The subtitle was also updated to **The Faces, Moments, and Culture Behind Our Growth.**

**Reasoning:** The Swiper-based layout improves image visibility, responsiveness, and navigation compared to the previous fan-style arrangement. By presenting photos in a clean, structured carousel, visitors can browse team moments more comfortably while enjoying a smoother experience across desktop and mobile devices.
